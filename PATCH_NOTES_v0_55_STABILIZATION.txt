GAMEPLAY FEEL IMPROVEMENTS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- Added HITSTOP: Clean hits now freeze both fighters for 3-10 frames depending
  on attack type (light=3, heavy=6, special=10). Blocked hits get 2 frames.
  This gives every connection a visceral "impact" feel.
- Added CAMERA SHAKE: The fight scene shakes on every hit, with intensity
  scaling by attack type. KO hits trigger a large 20-frame shake.
- Added screen flash overlay during hitstop for visual emphasis.
- Moved all attack properties into a centralized ATTACK_DATA table:
    light:   startup=4, active=6, recovery=4, dmg=8, range=54
    heavy:   startup=8, active=8, recovery=7, dmg=15, range=72
    special: startup=12, active=12, recovery=7, dmg=22, range=105
  Each attack also has: knockback, launch, meterGain, hitstun, blockstun, chipDmg.
- Rewrote CPU AI with a 5-state machine: approach, attack, block, retreat, spacing.
  The AI now transitions states based on game situation (enemy attacking, low HP,
  distance, meter availability) instead of purely random decisions.
  - Block state: CPU actively guards when enemy attacks
  - Retreat state: CPU backs away when at low HP
  - Spacing state: CPU maintains mid-range and punishes whiffs
  - Attack state: CPU commits to offensive pressure
  - Approach state: CPU closes distance to engage
